About

About Abhishek

Abhishek Kumar Jha is an Assistant Professor of Information Management & Analytics at SPJIMR, Mumbai.

He studies how people and organisations work with AI, using machine learning, language models and experiments. He teaches generative AI, agentic AI and analytics to MBA students and executives, and research methods to doctoral scholars.

Previously a Lecturer at Deakin University. PhD, IIM Indore.
