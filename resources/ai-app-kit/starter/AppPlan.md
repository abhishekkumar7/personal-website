# App plan

Fill this in before you ask your AI assistant to build anything. This file is your instruction to it.
Keep it short and concrete. A basic first version is the goal; you will improve it later.

## 1. App idea
- **Name (working title):**
- **Use case:** What problem does it help with, in one sentence?
- **Intended user:** Who opens this app, and what do they already know?
- **User input:** What does the person type, choose or upload?
- **Model output:** What should the answer look like (length, format, tone)?
- **Model approach:** Just a good system prompt? Or answers based on source material you provide (for example a policy document or a small data file)?
- **Source material or data:** What do you need to supply? Synthetic data is fine for a demo.
